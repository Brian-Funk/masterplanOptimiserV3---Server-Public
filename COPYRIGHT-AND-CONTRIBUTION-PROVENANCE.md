## Future contributions

Contributors must submit only work they have the right to contribute and agree
that their contribution is provided under `AGPL-3.0-only`. A contribution must
not include secrets, personal data, internal engineering notes or material
copied under incompatible terms. Dependency updates do not transfer ownership
of the dependency and must update the generated notices when applicable.
